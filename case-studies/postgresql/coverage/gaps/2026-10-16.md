## Suggested order

1. EXPLAIN plus a view/deparse oracle: the largest block (~14k), cheap, and
   ruleutils' deparse-and-reparse is a known source of real bugs.
2. SRFs in FROM, LATERAL, MERGE / ON CONFLICT / RETURNING: cheap, model-checkable.
3. Range and multirange types, then extended statistics.
4. Foreign keys and triggers, then ALTER TABLE breadth: the biggest model
   changes, the biggest correctness payoff.

Before a scored round: held-out monitors, a control (what any session reaches),
scorer checksums, and the instrumented build on the run's master
(postgresql-coverage/README.md, "Not yet").
